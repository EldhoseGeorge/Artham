Privacy Policy

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit personal user data to any external server.

The extension may use local browser storage to remember simple preferences such as whether a specific website should be enabled or disabled for dictionary lookup.

No account creation, login, analytics, or external data sharing is required for this extension to function.

The only information processed is the selected word text on the webpage, which is used locally to retrieve and display dictionary meanings within the extension UI.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact the developer through the extension listing or project support channel.
